# thai Style Cake with carrots Filling

UUID: cdb3f123-5b1b-4b36-8f6f-3baf9731a036


A delicious thai dish combining fish with fresh carrots served with rice

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 14 minutes
Servings: 5
Tags: dessert, thai, day-68, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fish
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare fish by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook fish until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
