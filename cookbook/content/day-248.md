# caribbean pork with mushrooms and couscous

A delicious caribbean dish combining pork with fresh mushrooms served with couscous

Prep Time: 29 minutes
Cook Time: 60 minutes
Servings: 3
Tags: dinner, caribbean, day-248

## Ingredients

- 1 lb pork
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare pork by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook pork until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
