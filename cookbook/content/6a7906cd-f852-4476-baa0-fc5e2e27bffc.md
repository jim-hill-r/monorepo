# Fresh zucchini Salad with lamb

UUID: 6a7906cd-f852-4476-baa0-fc5e2e27bffc


A delicious thai dish combining lamb with fresh zucchini served with noodles

Prep Time: 8 minutes
Cook Time: 33 minutes
Servings: 6
Tags: salad, thai, day-350, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb lamb
- 2 cups zucchini
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare lamb by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook lamb until golden
4. Add zucchini and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
