# Scrambled Eggs with Herbs

UUID: 341c15d1-9fef-4cf7-b594-7cc33b6ad867


Creamy scrambled eggs with fresh herbs

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 5 minutes
Servings: 2
Tags: breakfast, american, day-71, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 1 tablespoon butter
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons fresh herbs (chives, parsley)

## Instructions

1. Whisk eggs and milk together
2. Melt butter in pan over medium-low heat
3. Pour in eggs and gently stir
4. Cook until softly set, about 3-4 minutes
5. Stir in herbs and season with salt and pepper
