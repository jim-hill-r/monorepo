# mexican pork Wrap with mushrooms

A delicious mexican dish combining pork with fresh mushrooms served with noodles

Prep Time: 29 minutes
Cook Time: 57 minutes
Servings: 3
Tags: lunch, mexican, day-212

## Ingredients

- 1 lb pork
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare pork by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook pork until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
