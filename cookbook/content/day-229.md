# Banana Bread

Moist and flavorful banana bread

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 60 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: breakfast, american, day-229, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe bananas
- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Mash bananas in a bowl
3. Mix in eggs, butter, and sugar
4. Add flour, baking soda, and salt
5. Pour into greased loaf pan
6. Bake for 60 minutes
