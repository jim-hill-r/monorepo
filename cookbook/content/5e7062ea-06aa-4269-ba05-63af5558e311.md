# Greek Yogurt Parfait

UUID: 5e7062ea-06aa-4269-ba05-63af5558e311


Layered parfait with Greek yogurt, granola, and berries

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 2
Tags: breakfast, greek, day-32, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Greek yogurt
- 1 cup granola
- 2 cups mixed berries
- 2 tablespoons honey

## Instructions

1. Layer yogurt in glasses or bowls
2. Add a layer of granola
3. Top with mixed berries
4. Drizzle with honey
5. Repeat layers and serve immediately
