# chinese tofu Wrap with mushrooms

A delicious chinese dish combining tofu with fresh mushrooms served with noodles

Prep Time: 11 minutes
Cook Time: 36 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: lunch, chinese, day-220, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb tofu
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare tofu by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook tofu until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
