# Breakfast Burrito

Hearty breakfast burrito with eggs and vegetables

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: breakfast, mexican, day-309, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- 4 large tortillas
- 1 cup black beans
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- Salsa for serving

## Instructions

1. Scramble eggs in a pan
2. Sauté bell pepper and onion until soft
3. Warm tortillas
4. Fill tortillas with eggs, vegetables, beans, and cheese
5. Roll up and serve with salsa
