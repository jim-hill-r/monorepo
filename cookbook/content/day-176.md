# american tofu Skewers

A delicious american dish combining tofu with fresh spinach served with rice

Prep Time: 17 minutes
Cook Time: 13 minutes
Servings: 7
Tags: appetizer, american, day-176, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb tofu
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare tofu by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook tofu until golden
4. Add spinach and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
