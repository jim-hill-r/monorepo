# caribbean Style zucchini Smoothie

A delicious caribbean dish combining tofu with fresh zucchini served with couscous

Prep Time: 22 minutes
Cook Time: 11 minutes
Servings: 5
Tags: beverage, caribbean, day-14, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb tofu
- 2 cups zucchini
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare tofu by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook tofu until golden
4. Add zucchini and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
