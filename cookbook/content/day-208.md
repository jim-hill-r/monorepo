# caribbean pork Wrap with carrots

A delicious caribbean dish combining pork with fresh carrots served with pasta

Prep Time: 24 minutes
Cook Time: 43 minutes
Servings: 5
Tags: lunch, caribbean, day-208

## Ingredients

- 1 lb pork
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare pork by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook pork until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
