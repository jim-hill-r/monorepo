# mexican pork with carrots and couscous

UUID: 2dc57fde-e08b-4dcb-ad3f-fb413c91fb6c


A delicious mexican dish combining pork with fresh carrots served with couscous

Prep Time: 12 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, mexican, day-240

## Ingredients

- 1 lb pork
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare pork by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook pork until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
