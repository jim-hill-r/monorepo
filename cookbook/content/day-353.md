# Japanese Tamago

UUID: 9634570e-87f5-48f5-a142-872fbf1b9a6f


Sweet Japanese rolled omelette

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: breakfast, japanese, day-353, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons mirin
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Beat eggs with sugar, soy sauce, and mirin
2. Heat oiled pan over medium heat
3. Pour thin layer of egg mixture
4. Roll egg when partially set
5. Repeat with remaining mixture
6. Slice and serve
