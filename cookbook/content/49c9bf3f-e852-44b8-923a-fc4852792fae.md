# Crispy mushrooms Bites

UUID: 49c9bf3f-e852-44b8-923a-fc4852792fae


A delicious japanese dish combining lamb with fresh mushrooms served with noodles

Prep Time: 17 minutes
Cook Time: 53 minutes
Servings: 7
Tags: snack, japanese, day-121, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb lamb
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare lamb by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook lamb until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
