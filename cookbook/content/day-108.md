# Spaghetti Carbonara

UUID: 963df110-0ebf-4aeb-aa44-5119768a2dbe


Classic Roman pasta with eggs, cheese, and pancetta

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, italian, day-108, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 400g spaghetti
- 200g pancetta
- 4 eggs
- 100g Pecorino Romano
- Black pepper
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Cook spaghetti in salted water
2. Fry pancetta until crispy
3. Whisk eggs with grated cheese and pepper
4. Drain pasta, reserve 1 cup pasta water
5. Toss hot pasta with pancetta off heat
6. Add egg mixture and toss quickly
7. Add pasta water as needed for creamy sauce
