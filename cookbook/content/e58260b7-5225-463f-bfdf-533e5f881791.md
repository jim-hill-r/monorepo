# Classic American Pancakes

UUID: e58260b7-5225-463f-bfdf-533e5f881791


Fluffy buttermilk pancakes perfect for a weekend breakfast

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: breakfast, american, day-294, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 3/4 cups buttermilk
- 1/4 cup melted butter

## Instructions

1. Mix dry ingredients in a large bowl
2. Whisk eggs, buttermilk, and melted butter in another bowl
3. Combine wet and dry ingredients until just mixed
4. Heat griddle and cook pancakes for 2-3 minutes per side
5. Serve with maple syrup and butter
