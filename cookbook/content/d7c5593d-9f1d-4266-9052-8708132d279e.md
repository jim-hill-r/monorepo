# Tiramisu

UUID: d7c5593d-9f1d-4266-9052-8708132d279e


Classic Italian coffee-flavored dessert

Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: dessert, italian, day-120, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 24 ladyfinger cookies
- 2 cups strong espresso
- 1 lb mascarpone cheese
- 4 eggs, separated
- 1/2 cup sugar
- Cocoa powder
- Vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Beat egg yolks with sugar until thick
2. Fold in mascarpone
3. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks
4. Fold egg whites into mascarpone mixture
5. Dip ladyfingers in espresso
6. Layer cookies and cream mixture
7. Dust with cocoa powder
8. Refrigerate 4 hours
