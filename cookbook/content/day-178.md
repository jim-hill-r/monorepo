# Chicken Stir Fry

Quick and healthy chicken stir fry with vegetables

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 12 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, chinese, day-178, quick

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, sliced
- 2 cups mixed vegetables
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 inch ginger
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in wok over high heat
2. Stir fry chicken until cooked
3. Remove chicken
4. Stir fry vegetables with garlic and ginger
5. Return chicken to wok
6. Add sauces and sesame oil
7. Toss and serve over rice
