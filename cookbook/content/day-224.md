# american Style carrots Smoothie

A delicious american dish combining lamb with fresh carrots served with couscous

Prep Time: 23 minutes
Cook Time: 28 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: beverage, american, day-224, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb lamb
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare lamb by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook lamb until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
