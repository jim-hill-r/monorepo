# Eggs Benedict

Classic eggs Benedict with hollandaise sauce

Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: breakfast, american, day-254, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 English muffins
- 8 eggs
- 8 slices Canadian bacon
- 3 egg yolks
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and cayenne

## Instructions

1. Toast English muffins
2. Poach eggs
3. Cook Canadian bacon
4. Make hollandaise: whisk yolks with lemon juice, slowly add melted butter
5. Assemble: muffin, bacon, egg, hollandaise
6. Serve immediately
