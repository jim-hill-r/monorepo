# french beef Wrap with bell peppers

A delicious french dish combining beef with fresh bell peppers served with noodles

Prep Time: 12 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: lunch, french, day-253

## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef
- 2 cups bell peppers
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare beef by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook beef until golden
4. Add bell peppers and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
