# indian beef Breakfast Bowl

UUID: 310367c8-3fc7-4b94-9d01-95db5ca888a5


A delicious indian dish combining beef with fresh carrots served with rice

Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes
Servings: 5
Tags: breakfast, indian, day-152

## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare beef by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook beef until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
