# Beef Tacos

UUID: 483bfdac-d263-4f7a-8d57-d738be174ee1


Seasoned ground beef tacos with toppings

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 6
Tags: dinner, mexican, day-316, quick

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- Taco seasoning
- 12 taco shells
- Lettuce, shredded
- Tomatoes, diced
- Cheese, shredded
- Sour cream
- Salsa

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in pan
2. Add taco seasoning and water
3. Simmer until thickened
4. Warm taco shells
5. Fill shells with beef
6. Top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, and salsa
