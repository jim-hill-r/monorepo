# Fresh spinach Salad with chicken

A delicious italian dish combining chicken with fresh spinach served with couscous

Prep Time: 24 minutes
Cook Time: 17 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: salad, italian, day-184

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare chicken by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook chicken until golden
4. Add spinach and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
