# Tuna Salad Sandwich

Classic tuna salad on toasted bread

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: lunch, american, day-298, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 2 cans tuna, drained
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 8 slices bread
- Lettuce leaves

## Instructions

1. Mix tuna, mayo, celery, and lemon juice
2. Toast bread
3. Spread tuna salad on bread
4. Add lettuce
5. Top with second slice
