# mediterranean beef with bell peppers and pasta

UUID: f16d4589-05a6-48de-8508-efe8cd750aa6


A delicious mediterranean dish combining beef with fresh bell peppers served with pasta

Prep Time: 12 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, mediterranean, day-74, quick

## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef
- 2 cups bell peppers
- 1 cup pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare beef by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook beef until golden
4. Add bell peppers and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
