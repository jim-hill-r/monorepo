# caribbean beef Wrap with bell peppers

UUID: f6d0ae81-d111-47fe-8139-bd41fff4b0c3


A delicious caribbean dish combining beef with fresh bell peppers served with couscous

Prep Time: 16 minutes
Cook Time: 24 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: lunch, caribbean, day-25

## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef
- 2 cups bell peppers
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare beef by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook beef until golden
4. Add bell peppers and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
