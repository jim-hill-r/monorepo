# Classic BLT Sandwich

UUID: 1799fb88-7012-47f2-8781-136cf284232e


Bacon, lettuce, and tomato sandwich with mayo

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: lunch, american, day-141, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 slices bread
- 12 slices bacon
- 2 large tomatoes, sliced
- Lettuce leaves
- Mayonnaise

## Instructions

1. Cook bacon until crispy
2. Toast bread slices
3. Spread mayo on bread
4. Layer with bacon, lettuce, and tomato
5. Top with second slice and serve
