# French Toast

Crispy golden French toast with cinnamon

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: breakfast, french, day-67, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 slices bread
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 2 tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Whisk together eggs, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon
2. Dip bread slices in egg mixture
3. Melt butter in pan over medium heat
4. Cook bread for 2-3 minutes per side until golden
5. Serve with powdered sugar and syrup
