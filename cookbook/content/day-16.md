# Fresh mushrooms Salad with tofu

A delicious vietnamese dish combining tofu with fresh mushrooms served with pasta

Prep Time: 12 minutes
Cook Time: 46 minutes
Servings: 2
Tags: salad, vietnamese, day-16, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb tofu
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare tofu by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook tofu until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
