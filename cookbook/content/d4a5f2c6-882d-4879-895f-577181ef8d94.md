# korean beef Skewers

UUID: d4a5f2c6-882d-4879-895f-577181ef8d94


A delicious korean dish combining beef with fresh mushrooms served with noodles

Prep Time: 19 minutes
Cook Time: 18 minutes
Servings: 3
Tags: appetizer, korean, day-302

## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare beef by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook beef until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
