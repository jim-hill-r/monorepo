# Grilled Salmon

Perfectly grilled salmon with lemon and herbs

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, mediterranean, day-49, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets
- 2 lemons
- Fresh dill
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- Garlic cloves

## Instructions

1. Preheat grill to medium-high
2. Brush salmon with olive oil
3. Season with salt, pepper, and minced garlic
4. Grill skin-side down for 6 minutes
5. Flip and grill 4 more minutes
6. Top with fresh dill and lemon juice
