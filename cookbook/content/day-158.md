# french pork Soup with carrots

UUID: 02512f78-c3c4-4ad6-a080-c8953253b3c2


A delicious french dish combining pork with fresh carrots served with rice

Prep Time: 26 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: soup, french, day-158

## Ingredients

- 1 lb pork
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare pork by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook pork until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
