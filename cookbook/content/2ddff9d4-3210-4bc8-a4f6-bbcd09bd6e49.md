# caribbean fish Soup with mushrooms

UUID: 2ddff9d4-3210-4bc8-a4f6-bbcd09bd6e49


A delicious caribbean dish combining fish with fresh mushrooms served with couscous

Prep Time: 12 minutes
Cook Time: 55 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: soup, caribbean, day-167, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fish
- 2 cups mushrooms
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare fish by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook fish until golden
4. Add mushrooms and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
