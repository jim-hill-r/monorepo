# Avocado Toast

UUID: c87d086d-15da-4e16-8a90-106829547219


Simple and nutritious avocado toast

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 5 minutes
Servings: 2
Tags: breakfast, american, day-219, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 slices whole grain bread
- 2 ripe avocados
- Lemon juice
- Salt and pepper
- Red pepper flakes
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Toast bread until golden
2. Mash avocados with lemon juice, salt, and pepper
3. Spread avocado mixture on toast
4. Drizzle with olive oil
5. Sprinkle with red pepper flakes
