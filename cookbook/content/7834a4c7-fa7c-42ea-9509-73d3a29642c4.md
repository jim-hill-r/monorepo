# caribbean shrimp Soup with bell peppers

UUID: 7834a4c7-fa7c-42ea-9509-73d3a29642c4


A delicious caribbean dish combining shrimp with fresh bell peppers served with rice

Prep Time: 6 minutes
Cook Time: 14 minutes
Servings: 6
Tags: soup, caribbean, day-144, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp
- 2 cups bell peppers
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare shrimp by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook shrimp until golden
4. Add bell peppers and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
