# chinese Style spinach Smoothie

UUID: 80946710-1d7d-4f72-937c-8b78dd6df01c


A delicious chinese dish combining shrimp with fresh spinach served with pasta

Prep Time: 16 minutes
Cook Time: 60 minutes
Servings: 8
Tags: beverage, chinese, day-195, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare shrimp by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook shrimp until golden
4. Add spinach and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
