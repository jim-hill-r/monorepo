# japanese fish Skewers

UUID: c0c07392-d587-48b0-820f-745c26262b78


A delicious japanese dish combining fish with fresh carrots served with couscous

Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 32 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: appetizer, japanese, day-275, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fish
- 2 cups carrots
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare fish by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook fish until golden
4. Add carrots and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
