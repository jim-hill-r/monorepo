# vietnamese tofu Soup with broccoli

UUID: 257762f2-675d-41ef-b588-f5c3182ad325


A delicious vietnamese dish combining tofu with fresh broccoli served with rice

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 47 minutes
Servings: 6
Tags: soup, vietnamese, day-79, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb tofu
- 2 cups broccoli
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare tofu by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook tofu until golden
4. Add broccoli and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
