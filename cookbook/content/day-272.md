# Breakfast Quesadilla

Cheesy quesadilla filled with scrambled eggs

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 8 minutes
Servings: 2
Tags: breakfast, mexican, day-272, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 4 tortillas
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 2 tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Scramble eggs
2. Place cheese and eggs on half of each tortilla
3. Fold tortillas in half
4. Cook in buttered pan until golden and cheese melts
5. Serve with salsa
