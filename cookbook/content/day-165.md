# Crispy zucchini Bites

UUID: 42ca3ef9-4ca0-4895-8742-08d1d99855b5


A delicious french dish combining lamb with fresh zucchini served with quinoa

Prep Time: 14 minutes
Cook Time: 43 minutes
Servings: 7
Tags: snack, french, day-165, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb lamb
- 2 cups zucchini
- 1 cup quinoa
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare lamb by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook lamb until golden
4. Add zucchini and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook quinoa according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
