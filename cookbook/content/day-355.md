# indian Style Cake with zucchini Filling

UUID: 4b9865a2-6469-41eb-a208-c2946827402d


A delicious indian dish combining beef with fresh zucchini served with rice

Prep Time: 13 minutes
Cook Time: 34 minutes
Servings: 7
Tags: dessert, indian, day-355

## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef
- 2 cups zucchini
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare beef by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook beef until golden
4. Add zucchini and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
