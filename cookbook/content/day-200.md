# Crispy broccoli Bites

UUID: 7adfd21b-15d5-4c70-be87-0f6691f34938


A delicious american dish combining shrimp with fresh broccoli served with couscous

Prep Time: 27 minutes
Cook Time: 56 minutes
Servings: 7
Tags: snack, american, day-200, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp
- 2 cups broccoli
- 1 cup couscous
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare shrimp by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook shrimp until golden
4. Add broccoli and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook couscous according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
