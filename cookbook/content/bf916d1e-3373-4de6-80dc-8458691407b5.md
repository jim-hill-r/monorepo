# Caesar Salad

UUID: bf916d1e-3373-4de6-80dc-8458691407b5


Classic Caesar salad with homemade dressing

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: salad, italian, day-94, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 head romaine lettuce
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 cup croutons
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 anchovy fillets
- 1 egg yolk
- Lemon juice
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Chop romaine lettuce
2. Make dressing: blend garlic, anchovies, egg yolk, lemon juice
3. Slowly add olive oil while blending
4. Toss lettuce with dressing
5. Top with Parmesan and croutons
