# Oatmeal with Berries

Warm and comforting oatmeal topped with fresh berries

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 2
Tags: breakfast, american, day-54, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 cups milk or water
- Pinch of salt
- 1 cup mixed berries
- 2 tablespoons honey
- Cinnamon to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring milk or water to a boil
2. Add oats and salt
3. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes
4. Top with berries, honey, and cinnamon
5. Serve hot
