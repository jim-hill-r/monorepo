# indian shrimp with bell peppers and rice

UUID: 81292dae-af66-4107-82c7-26baca270d2d


A delicious indian dish combining shrimp with fresh bell peppers served with rice

Prep Time: 29 minutes
Cook Time: 46 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, indian, day-245, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp
- 2 cups bell peppers
- 1 cup rice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare shrimp by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook shrimp until golden
4. Add bell peppers and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
