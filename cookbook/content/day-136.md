# korean fish Wrap with spinach

A delicious korean dish combining fish with fresh spinach served with noodles

Prep Time: 17 minutes
Cook Time: 29 minutes
Servings: 5
Tags: lunch, korean, day-136, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fish
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Prepare fish by cutting into bite-sized pieces
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Cook fish until golden
4. Add spinach and garlic, sauté until tender
5. Meanwhile, cook noodles according to package directions
6. Combine everything and season with salt and pepper
7. Serve hot and enjoy
