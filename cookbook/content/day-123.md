# Chicken Noodle Soup

Comforting homemade chicken noodle soup

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes
Servings: 6
Tags: soup, american, day-123

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cups egg noodles
- Fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. Boil chicken breasts in broth until cooked
2. Remove and shred chicken
3. Add vegetables to broth and simmer
4. Add noodles and cook until tender
5. Return chicken to soup
6. Garnish with parsley
