# Sparkhill user guide

Sparkhill has two things people use: **Blue Eel**, a free app for practising
writing letters and words, and the **Spark Hill website**, which tells
families about Spark Hill's reading tutoring. This page gets you started
with both; each has its own page with the details.

- [Using Blue Eel](blue-eel.md): for learners, parents and teachers.
- [Using the Spark Hill website](website.md): for families looking for a
  reading tutor.

## Who it's for

- **Blue Eel** is for learners, often children, including children with
  dyslexia, who are learning to write, and for the parents and teachers
  who sit with them. It shows how each letter is written, then checks the
  learner's own tracing. There is no account and nothing a learner types
  or draws leaves the device.
- **The website** is for families and adults who want to know what Spark
  Hill does, who runs it and how to get in touch.

## Getting started

- **Blue Eel:** open <https://jimhillr-sparkhill-webapp.pages.dev> on a
  tablet, phone or computer and press **Begin**. A tablet with a finger or
  stylus works best. You can add it to your home screen to open it like an
  app. [Using Blue Eel](blue-eel.md#getting-started) has the first steps.
- **The website:** open <https://jimhillr-sparkhill.pages.dev>. It is one
  page; scroll down or use the links at the top.

## Getting help

- Blue Eel's common problems (a letter that keeps saying "Try again!",
  progress that seems lost, a page that won't zoom) are answered in
  [Using Blue Eel](blue-eel.md#getting-help).
- The website's are in [Using the Spark Hill website](website.md#getting-help).
- To ask a question or report a problem with either, press **Contact Us**
  at the top of the website. It opens Spark Hill's Nextdoor page, which
  takes messages. Say which page you were on, what you pressed and what
  happened. Blue Eel has no feedback button, because it sends nothing
  anywhere.
