# Using the Spark Hill website

The Spark Hill website introduces Spark Hill's reading tutoring: what it
does, who runs it and how it works, with a way to get in touch.

## Who it's for

Families and adults looking for help with reading, including help with
dyslexia, who want to know whether Spark Hill fits before they get in
touch.

## Getting started

Open <https://jimhillr-sparkhill.pages.dev> in any browser. The site is one
page in English (its address ends in `/en-US/`), and there is nothing to
sign in to.

## How to find something on the website

The page has four sections, in this order as you scroll: **What we do**,
**Who we are**, **How we do it** and **Testimonials**.

- **On a computer**, the links at the top (**What we do**, **Who we are**,
  **How we do it**) jump to their section. **Spark Hill** at the top left
  goes back to the top.
- **On a phone**, those links are hidden to save space: scroll down to the
  section instead.
- **To share a section**, copy its link, such as
  `https://jimhillr-sparkhill.pages.dev/en-US/#how-we-do-it`. It opens the
  page at that section.
- **To get in touch**, press **Contact Us** at the top or at the bottom of
  the page. It opens Spark Hill's Nextdoor page in a new tab, where you can
  send a message. **Nextdoor** under Testimonials opens the same page,
  with reviews.
- The links at the very bottom go to other organisations that help with
  reading and attention: dyslexiaida.org, CHADD.org and ASHA.org, and to
  the original Blue Eel app at blue.eel.education. Our own copy of Blue Eel
  is described in [Using Blue Eel](blue-eel.md).

## Getting help

- **A section link opened the top of the page.** Scroll down to the
  section, or reload the page. (The site sometimes misses the jump; it is
  being fixed.)
- **Contact Us didn't open anything.** It opens in a new tab: check that
  your browser didn't block it, or go to Nextdoor and search for "Private
  Reading Tutor Oceanside CA".
- **A page says it can't be found.** The site has only its home page;
  press **Spark Hill** or open <https://jimhillr-sparkhill.pages.dev>.

To ask a question or report a problem with the site, send a message through
**Contact Us**. Say what you pressed and what happened.
