Goal: Maximize your society's welfare without dying to existential crisis or communal destitute.

Deck:
52 Action Cards
- 13 Technology Cards (Blue)
- 13 Government Cards (Red)
- 13 Environment Cards (Green)
- 13 Economy Cards (Yellow)
13 Crisis Cards (Problems for everyone)
13 Profession Cards (Roles a player can be)
13 Civilian Cards (Support for one community, requires upkeep)
13 Society Cards (Support for everyone)

Losing: 
- Everybody loses if a global crisis is not averted.
- One player automatically loses if they have no civilians left

Winning:
- The remaining player with the highest civilian happiness score wins.

Mechanics:
Actions
- Each action card contains an action and a passive. Both may or may not power up when additional cards are placed below it.
- Back of each card has four power ups as well depending on how you place it.
- Up to four cards can power up a base card. It can be placed just above, below, left adjacent or right adjacent. Depending on the position different enhancements will be bestowed.
- Each color will have 1 card with an extra powerful synergy with another color.

Society/Crisis
- Each crisis card has a matching society card to prevent it.
- Society cards are necessary to prevent crisis, but also have benefits for all communities meeting specific requirements.

Civilians
- Each player starts with two. One face up, one face down. You use this to track overall happiness.
- More can be earn through various mechanics.
- Each card has a population on it and it's upkeep. When it's face up, u can include that population for action requirements.
- If happiness get's too low, bad things can happen based on action/society cards showing.
- Some actions require high levels of happiness.

Play
- Shuffle each type of card.
- Slide one random crisis card approximately halfway through the deck
- Put one crisis card on the bottom of the deck.
- Remove one society card a random and exclude it from the game. If 3 players, remove 2.
- Deal everyone 3 profession cards. Discard one and exclude it from the game.
- Deal everyone 3 action cards.
- Each player then takes his/her turn
- - Verify you meet upkeep of your community, else take action on citizen card
- - Perform all start of turn passives
- - Make a choice:
- - - Draw a card from the action pile (if u have 5 or fewer cards)
- - - Draw a card from the society pile (if u have 5 or fewer cards)
- - - Place a card out of your hand in play
- - - Play an action on a card in play. At least two action cards of the same color must be in play to use this option.
- - - - Once action is complete, put action card underneath another card in play of the same color. So that it is face up showing it's bonus. The card need not be your own card. The top card now gets both the played card bonus and it's own bonus.
- - Perform all end of turn passives
- Game ends when deck runs out.
