# 0008. v1 covers the rest of the 2026 regular season

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-25

## Context

Planning started during week 13 of the 2026 season, with about five weeks of
the regular season left.

## Decision

- v1 aims to be live for as much of the rest of the 2026 regular season as
  possible.
- Only the current week's guide is shown. There is no archive of past weeks.
- Rooting during the playoffs is out of scope for v1.
- The project is named `bear_down`.

## Consequences

- Playoff-week guides and a weekly archive are backlog items in `ISSUES.md`.
- The simulator still simulates the playoffs, because Super Bowl odds depend
  on them.
